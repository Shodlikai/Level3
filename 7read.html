Modern Cowboys

Cowboys all lived in the 19th century, when the Wild West really was wild. There are no cowboys in the USA in the 21st century. Right? No, wrong! Even today, there are still men and women who work as cowboys and cowgirls in the western USA and in Canada.... and in South America too!

Are there still cowboys in the American West? The answer is certainly yes! There are still thousands of cowboys in the American west. They still ride horses, and they still carry guns; but they no longer spend time fighting Indians. Although they often travel on four wheels, not four legs, cowboys still have to be expert horsemen too. They ride horses very regularly. Some of the ranches in the American West cover thousands of hectares. Cattle are often free to move over large areas. There are steep hills, rivers and streams, forests and rocks. In many places, it is impossible to use a pick-up, even with four-wheel drive; even motorbikes are useless.

The only ways to cross the prairies are on horseback, or on foot; and you can't chase cattle on foot!

Cowboys are working men; they look after the herds of cattle that live on the prairies in the mountains, from Texas to Canada. They have lots of jobs to do; they have to mark cattle so that they can be identified; they have to move cattle from place to place; they have to vaccinate them, and check that they are healthy. They also have to inspect miles and miles of fences and barbed-wire barriers.

There is no shortage of people who want to be cowboys. The profession has a very special reputation; it is different from other jobs, but it can be hard work, with long hours. Besides, many cowboys are not very well paid. It can also be a dangerous job.

Cowboys do not often fall off their horses, of course! They have to be excellent riders; but they carry guns for two reasons. Firstly, they may have to kill an injured animal - a cow or even a horse; secondly, they may encounter dangerous wild animals, such as rattle snakes. There are bears and wolves too, but they do not usually attack humans. Nevertheless, it is useful to have a gun, just in case.....

Fact: Cowboys used to herd thousands of cattle across long distances (sometimes over 1000 miles) from Texas to railroads in Kansas and Missouri.

CHANGING COWBOYS

In the 19th century, cowboys used to drive cattle for hundreds of miles. Sometimes they had to take them to new destinations; sometimes they had to take them to market. Today, there are no more long cattle drives. These days, cattle are picked up in big trucks and taken to new destinations by road.

That is one thing that has changed. Another more modern idea is the "dude ranch".

Anyone who wants to can become a cowboy for a week or more, if he has the money. Dude ranches offer holidays to people who want to be part-time cowboys! Of course, dudes don't do much real work, but they enjoy the spirit of the west.

Will there be Life after Oil and Gas ?

Today, we use a lot of oil and gas, but we cannot use them forever. There is enough oil and gas for a few more decades-maybe until 2070. After that, most of the oil will be gone. This is a big problem, but there is another problem too: climate change. The Earth is getting hotter because we burn too much oil, gas, and coal. Right now, there is still a lot of oil and gas under the ground and under the sea. Engineers keep finding new oil in places like the North Sea and the Atlantic Ocean. But we must understand some important facts:

Oil and gas will not last forever. One day, they will run out.

Finding oil will become more difficult. It will also become more expensive than other types of energy.

Oil and gas cause pollution and make global warming worse.

But there is some good news too:

Scientists are making new types of oil from plants.

Some fuels already use plant oil. For example, bio-fuels contain sunflower oil.

The Future of Energy

Sunflower oil is cleaner than mineral oil, so it causes less pollution. But in the future, we may need all the land to grow food, not fuel. This means that vegetable oils may not be the best solution for energy. One day, we will have very little oil or no oil at all. Scientists are already working on new ideas. They are making cars, houses, and plastics that do not use oil. In the future, electricity will be the most important energy. But this electricity must be clean.

Right now, in Britain, electricity comes from different sources:

Some electricity is made from oil and gas, but oil power stations are closing because gas is expensive.

Nuclear power stations will also close because they cost too much, and people do not like them.

Fact: Even though only 30 countries use nuclear power, it still provides about 10% of the world's electricity.

What Will We Use Instead?

Gas is still important for now because it is cleaner than oil and easier to find. There is more gas than oil under the ground.

Gas can be used in cars and houses too. But a lot of the gas used in Europe comes from Russia. After the war in Ukraine, Europeans saw this as a big problem.

The Future: Renewable Energy

The sun, the wind, rivers, and oceans will give us all the energy we need!

It will be clean (no pollution).

It will last forever.

But we are not there yet! The 21st century will be a time of change. People over 50 years old today may not see a world without oil and gas. But young people under 20 might see it in their lifetime!

Kovboylar faqatgina 19-asrda, Yovvoyi G'arb chindan ham yovvoyi bo'lgan paytlarda yashagan. 21-asrda AQShda kovboylar yo'q. To'g'rimi? Yo'q, xato! Bugungi kunda ham AQSh g'arbida, Kanadada va hatto Janubiy Amerikada kovboy va kovboy ayollar sifatida ishlaydigan erkaklar va ayollar bor!

Amerika G'arbida hali ham kovboylar bormi? Javob shubhasiz ha! Amerika g'arbida hali ham minglab kovboylar mavjud. Ular hali ham ot minishadi va qurol olib yurishadi; ammo endi hindular bilan jang qilishga vaqt sarflashmaydi. Ular ko'pincha to'rt g'ildirakli transportda (mashinada) harakatlanishsa ham, kovboylar baribir mohir chavandoz bo'lishlari kerak. Ular juda muntazam ravishda ot minishadi. Amerika G'arbidagi ba'zi rancho (chorvachilik fermalari) minglab gektar maydonni egallaydi. Chorva mollari ko'pincha katta hududlarda erkin harakatlanadi. U yerlarda tik tepaliklar, daryolar va soylar, o'rmonlar va qoyalar bor. Ko'p joylarda, hatto to'rt g'ildirakli tortuvchi pikapdan ham foydalanishning ilojisi yo'q; hatto mototsikllar ham befoyda.

Yaylovlarni kesib o'tishning yagona yo'li otda yoki piyoda yurishdir; chorva mollarini esa piyoda quvishning ilojisi yo'q!

Kovboylar — mehnatkash odamlar; ular Texasdan Kanadagacha tog'lardagi yaylovlarda yashovchi chorva podalariga g'amxo'rlik qilishadi. Ularning bajaradigan ko'plab ishlari bor: mol-qo'ylarni aniqlash uchun ularga belgi qo'yishlari kerak; ularni joydan joyga ko'chirishlari kerak; ularni emlashlari va sog'lomligini tekshirishlari kerak. Shuningdek, ular milllab-milllab to'siqlar va tikanli sim to'siqlarni tekshirishlari kerak.

Kovboy bo'lishni istagan odamlar yetarlicha topiladi. Bu kasb juda o'ziga xos obro'ga ega; u boshqa ishlardan farq qiladi, ammo uzoq ish soatlari bilan og'ir mehnat bo'lishi mumkin. Bundan tashqari, ko'plab kovboylar juda yaxshi maosh olishmaydi. Bu xavfli ish ham bo'lishi mumkin.

Kovboylar, albatta, otlaridan tez-tez yiqilmaydilar! Ular ajoyib chavandoz bo'lishlari kerak; ammo ular ikki sababga ko'ra qurol olib yurishadi. Birinchidan, ular jarohatlangan hayvonni – sigirni yoki hatto otni – o'ldirishga majbur bo'lishlari mumkin; ikkinchidan, ular ilonlar kabi xavfli yovvoyi hayvonlarga duch kelishlari mumkin. Ayiqlar va bo'rilar ham bor, ammo ular odatda odamlarga hujum qilishmaydi. Shunga qaramay, ehtiyot shart bo'lsa, qurolga ega bo'lish foydalidir.....

Fakt: Kovboylar o'n minglab mol-qo'ylarni Texasdan Kanzasdagi Missuri temir yo'llarigacha (ba'zan 1000 mildan ortiq) uzoq masofalarga haydashardi.

O'ZGARUVCHAN KOVBOYLAR

19-asrda kovboylar yuzlab millab mol-qo'ylarni haydashardi. Ba'zan ularni yangi manzillarga olib borishlari kerak bo'lardi; ba'zan esa ularni bozorga olib borishardi. Bugungi kunda uzoq masofali chorva haydashlar yo'q. Hozirgi kunda chorva mollari katta yuk mashinalariga yuklanib, yo'l orqali yangi manzillarga olib boriladi.

Bu o'zgargan narsalardan biri. Yana bir zamonaviy g'oya "dude ranch" (dam olish rancho) hisoblanadi.

Pul topa olgan har bir kishi, bir hafta yoki undan ko'proq vaqtga kovboy bo'lishi mumkin. Dude ranchlar yarim kunlik kovboy bo'lishni istaganlarga dam olishni taklif qiladi! Albatta, "dudelar" ko'p haqiqiy ish qilmaydilar, ammo ular G'arb ruhidan zavqlanishadi.

Neft va gazdan keyin ham hayot bo'ladimi?

Bugun biz ko'p neft va gaz ishlatamiz, ammo ularni abadiy ishlatolmaymiz. Neft va gaz bir necha o'n yilliklar uchun yetarli – balki 2070 yilgacha. Undan keyin neftning aksariyati tugaydi. Bu katta muammo, ammo yana bir muammo bor: iqlim o'zgarishi. Yer isib bormoqda, chunki biz juda ko'p neft, gaz va ko'mir yoqamiz. Hozirda yer ostida va dengiz ostida hali ham ko'p neft va gaz mavjud. Muhandislar Shimoliy dengiz va Atlantika okeani kabi joylarda yangi neft konlarini topishmoqda. Ammo biz ba'zi muhim faktlarni tushunishimiz kerak:

Neft va gaz abadiy saqlanmaydi. Bir kun kelib ular tugaydi.

Neft topish qiyinlashadi. U boshqa energiya turlaridan qimmatroq ham bo'ladi.

Neft va gaz ifloslanishni keltirib chiqaradi va global isishni yomonlashtiradi.

Ammo yaxshi yangiliklar ham bor:

Olimlar o'simliklardan yangi turdagi neft ishlab chiqarishmoqda.

Ba'zi yoqilg'ilar allaqachon o'simlik moyini ishlatadi. Masalan, bioyoqilg'ilar kungaboqar moyini o'z ichiga oladi.
