Relative Clauses

Nisbiy olmoshlar (who, whom, whose, which, that) yordamida gaplarni bog'lashni o'rganamiz

Asosiy Qoidalar

Relative Clause nima?

Relative Clause (nisbiy ergash gap) biror otga (noun) qo'shimcha ma'lumot berish uchun ishlatiladi. Ular gaplarni yanada mazmunli va ravon qiladi. Bu ergash gaplar who, whom, whose, which, that kabi nisbiy olmoshlar bilan boshlanadi.

who / that → Insonlar uchun

Ta'riflanayotgan ot inson bo'lsa va shu ergash gapning egasi (subject) vazifasini bajarsa ishlatiladi.

which / that → Narsa va hayvonlar uchun

Ta'riflanayotgan ot narsa yoki hayvon bo'lsa ishlatiladi (ega yoki to'ldiruvchi sifatida).

whom → Insonlar uchun (To'ldiruvchi)

Ta'riflanayotgan ot inson bo'lsa va ergash gapning to'ldiruvchisi (object) vazifasini bajarsa ishlatiladi. Asosan rasmiy nutqda qo'llaniladi.

whose → Egalik uchun

Egalikni bildirish uchun ishlatiladi. Ham odamlarga, ham narsalarga nisbatan qo'llanilishi mumkin.

Olmoshlar orasidagi farqlar

WHO vs WHOM

Eng ko'p chalkashlik shu ikkisida bo'ladi. Asosiy qoida: who - ega (subject), whom - to'ldiruvchi (object).

Tekshirish usuli: Gapdagi olmosh o'rniga he/she qo'ya olsangiz - who. Agar him/her qo'ya olsangiz - whom.

OlmoshVazifasiMisolTushuntirish
who Ega (Subject) The man who called you is my boss. (He called you)
whom To'ldiruvchi (Object) The man whom you called is my boss. (You called him)

Eslatma: Hozirgi kunda, ayniqsa og'zaki nutqda, whom o'rniga ham who ishlatish keng tarqalgan. Lekin rasmiy yozishmalarda va imtihonlarda whom ni to'g'ri ishlatish muhim.

THAT vs WHO/WHICH

that juda universal olmosh bo'lib, ko'pincha who (insonlar uchun) va which (narsalar uchun) o'rnida kela oladi. Lekin uning o'z cheklovlari bor.

HolatWHO/WHICHTHATMisol
Defining Clauses (Aniqlovchi ergash gap) Mumkin Mumkin The book which/that I bought is on the table.
Non-Defining Clauses (Izohlovchi ergash gap, vergul bilan ajratiladi) Mumkin Mumkin emas My brother, who lives in London, is a doctor. (that yaramaydi)
Predlogdan (preposition) keyin Mumkin Mumkin emas The box in which I put the keys is lost. (in that yaramaydi)

Relative Clause Strukturalari (Ko'proq misollar bilan)

Ega sifatida (Subject Pronoun)

Ot + who/which/that + Fe'l...

1. The scientist who discovered the cure is famous.

2. I read a book which was written by a friend of mine.

3. The team that won the championship celebrated all night.

4. The children who play in the park are very noisy.

5. A computer is a machine which can perform calculations quickly.

To'ldiruvchi sifatida (Object Pronoun)

Ot + (whom/which/that) + Ega + Fe'l...

1. The movie (which) we watched last night was fantastic.

2. The woman (whom) you saw is my aunt.

3. Have you found the keys (that) you lost?

4. The song (which) they are listening to is very old.

5. The candidate (whom) we voted for won the election.

Egalik olmoshi (Possessive Pronoun)

Ot + whose + Ot + ...

1. That is the artist whose paintings are sold for millions.

2. I have a friend whose cat has its own Instagram account.

3. We met a couple whose children study abroad.

4. The car whose alarm was ringing belongs to my neighbor.

5. It's a story about a writer whose books were banned.

Har bir olmoshga oid ko'proq misollar

WHO

He is the doctor who saved my life.

The person who called you didn't leave a message.

I want to thank everyone who helped me.

The police are looking for the man who robbed the bank.

Any student who fails the exam must retake it.

WHICH

This is the phone which has the best camera.

The dog, which was very friendly, started wagging its tail.

She showed me the dress which she had bought.

The company, which was founded in 1990, is now a market leader.

He passed the exam, which surprised everybody.

up or

WHOM (Rasmiy)

The manager to whom I complained has been fired.

She is the lady with whom I traveled to Paris.

The students whom the principal praised were very happy.

This is my friend, about whom I have told you so much.

The candidates whom we interviewed were all highly qualified.

WHOSE

I saw a girl whose hair was incredibly long.

This is the book whose cover is torn.

He is a director whose films have won many awards.

The family whose house we bought is moving to Canada.

A country whose economy is strong attracts investors.

Mashqlar (5 xil turdagi)

To'g'ri olmoshni tanlang

1. My friend, ___ is a lawyer, gave me some advice.

2. The hotel ___ we stayed was very comfortable.

3. I know the woman ___ son is in your class.

Bo'sh joyni to'ldiring (who, which, whose, whom, where)

1. The reason ___ I'm calling is to invite you to a party.

2. This is the village ___ my grandfather was born.

3. Do you know the girl ___ is talking to Tom?

Ikki gapni bitta qilib birlashtiring

1. A man's wallet was stolen. He went to the police.

2. I bought a new car. It is very fast.

Xatoni toping va to'g'rilang

1. The person which stole your bag has been caught.

2. My mother, that is a great cook, is making dinner.

Qaysi gapda olmoshni tushirib qoldirish mumkin?

1. The dress which she is wearing is new.

2. The man who lives next door is a pilot.
