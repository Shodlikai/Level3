Hollywood - its life and times

Hollywood is one of the best-known names in the world: but where is Hollywood exactly? Why is it so successful? And how did it begin?

The Story of Hollywood

At the beginning of the 20th century, a new city started to grow in southern California near the sea. This city was called Los Angeles. The name came from an old Spanish mission that had been there for a long time.

At the same time, a new industry was born: cinema. In America, people first called it "motion pictures", but soon they started saying "movies" instead.

Why Did Hollywood Start in Los Angeles?

At first, movies were made in New York. But by 1910, many filmmakers moved to Los Angeles. Why?

New York was too expensive workers, land, and taxes cost a lot.

Winters in New York were too cold it was difficult to make movies in bad weather.

Los Angeles had good weather - filmmakers could work all year.

Land was cheap-there was lots of space for building movie studios.

A few miles outside Los Angeles, movie makers found a perfect place for their studios. Hollywood. Soon, large studios were built at the foot of the dry hills.

The Rise of Hollywood

Movies became very popular, especially after "talking movies" (movies with sound) appeared in 1925. However, making movies was expensive, so companies needed a lot of money. Because of this, a few big companies controlled Hollywood. These included:

  • MGM
  • 20th Century Fox
  • Warner Bros.
  • Paramount

Over the years, some companies disappeared, while new ones appeared, like Walt Disney and Columbia.

Fact: The famous Hollywood Sign was put up in 1923 and originally said "Hollywoodland" It was an advertisement for a real estate company. The "land" part was removed in 1949.

Hollywood Today

Hollywood has changed a lot in 100 years! Today, the biggest studios belong to huge international companies. For example, 21st Century Fox is part of Disney and owns many film and media companies around the world.

Movies today are very expensive. A big company like Fox had to work with Paramount to make Titanic, which was the most expensive movie at the time.

Hollywood no longer makes movies just for America. It now makes movies for the whole world! Los Angeles has become the center of a global dream industry.

Hollywood and Global Culture

Today, we live in a world with global culture. Hollywood did not create this culture, but it has become a big part of it. Whether we like it or not, Hollywood movies have changed the way people around the world dream and think.

Fact: The mountain logo of Paramount originally had 24 stars, representing the 24 actors who first signed contracts with the studio. Later, it was changed to 22 stars, but the exact reason remains a mystery!

The first bicycle

With covid and the coming climate crisis, bicycles are getting more and more popular. But where did the bicycle come from? Who invented this "velocipede"?.

The Story of the Bicycle

Did you know that the bicycle was invented after the railway locomotive?

It may be surprising, but the two-wheeler has come a long way since it was first made by a Scottish blacksmith, Kirkpatrick MacMillan, in 1839.

The First Bicycle

MacMillan got his idea from an older wooden vehicle called a "hobby horse." This vehicle had two wheels, and the rider had to push with their feet to move forward. However, it was not very safe because it had no steering and no brakes.

MacMillan, who was called "Mad Pate", made some important changes. He:

  • Added a system of moving bars the rider could push the bars with their feet to make the back wheel turn.
  • Made the front wheel turn-now the bike could be steered.

To show how his new bicycle worked, MacMillan rode 60 miles to Glasgow! It was a very difficult journey because the roads were bad, and the bike had no rubber tyres or springs.

How Bicycles Improved

MacMillan was not famous for his invention, but other people liked the idea of bicycles.

25 years later, a Frenchman named Pierre Lallemant designed the first bike with pedals.

In 1876, H.J. Lawson added the chain drive, which made bicycles much easier to ride.

Later, rubber tyres and gears were added, but the basic design of the bicycle has not changed much since then.

Fact: Studies show that regular cycling can add 3-5 years to your life! It also keeps your heart healthy and reduces stress.

The Bicycle Today

Today, the bicycle is one of the most popular types of transport in the world, especially in developing countries. It is cheap, does not pollute, and is easy to use.

In the future, bicycles could become the best way to travel in big cities. Thanks, Pate!

Hollywood - uning hayoti va davrlari

Hollywood dunyodagi eng mashhur nomlardan biri: ammo Hollywood aynan qayerda? Nima uchun u shunchalik muvaffaqiyatli? Va u qanday boshlangan?

Hollywood hikoyasi

20-asr boshlarida Janubiy Kaliforniyada dengiz yaqinida yangi shahar o'sa boshladi. Bu shahar Los-Anjeles deb nomlangan. Bu nom uzoq vaqtdan beri mavjud bo'lgan eski ispan missiyasidan kelib chiqqan.

Shu bilan birga, yangi sanoat tug'ildi: kino. Amerikada odamlar uni dastlab "harakatli rasmlar" deb atashgan, ammo tez orada "filmlar" deb ayta boshlashdi.

Nima uchun Hollywood Los-Anjelesda boshlangan?

Dastlab, filmlar Nyu-Yorkda suratga olingan. Ammo 1910 yilga kelib, ko'plab kinorejissyorlar Los-Anjelesga ko'chib o'tishdi. Nima uchun?

Nyu-York juda qimmat edi – ishchilar, yer va soliqlar juda ko'p turardi.

Nyu-Yorkdagi qishlar juda sovuq edi – yomon ob-havoda filmlar suratga olish qiyin edi.

Los-Anjelesda ob-havo yaxshi edi – kinorejissyorlar yil bo'yi ishlashlari mumkin edi.

Yer arzon edi – kinostudiyalar qurish uchun ko'p joy bor edi.

Los-Anjelesdan bir necha mil uzoqlikda kinorejissyorlar o'z studiyalari uchun mukammal joy topishdi. Hollywood. Tez orada quruq tepaliklar etagida katta studiyalar qurildi.

Hollywoodning yuksalishi

Filmlar juda mashhur bo'ldi, ayniqsa "gapiruvchi filmlar" (ovozli filmlar) 1925 yilda paydo bo'lgandan keyin. Biroq, filmlar suratga olish qimmat edi, shuning uchun kompaniyalarga juda ko'p pul kerak edi. Shu sababli, bir nechta yirik kompaniyalar Hollywoodni nazorat qildi. Bularga quyidagilar kiradi:

  • MGM
  • 20th Century Fox
  • Warner Bros.
  • Paramount

Yillar davomida ba'zi kompaniyalar yo'q bo'lib ketdi, boshqalari esa Walt Disney va Columbia kabi yangilari paydo bo'ldi.

Fakt: Mashhur Hollywood belgisi 1923 yilda o'rnatilgan va dastlab "Hollywoodland" deb yozilgan. Bu ko'chmas mulk kompaniyasi uchun reklama edi. "Land" qismi 1949 yilda olib tashlangan.

Hollywood bugun

Hollywood 100 yilda juda ko'p o'zgardi! Bugungi kunda eng yirik studiyalar yirik xalqaro kompaniyalarga tegishli. Masalan, 21st Century Fox Disney tarkibiga kiradi va butun dunyo bo'ylab ko'plab kino va media kompaniyalariga egalik qiladi.

Bugungi filmlar juda qimmat. Fox kabi yirik kompaniya o'sha paytda eng qimmat film bo'lgan "Titanik"ni suratga olish uchun Paramount bilan ishlashi kerak edi.

Hollywood endi faqat Amerika uchun filmlar suratga olmaydi. U endi butun dunyo uchun filmlar suratga oladi! Los-Anjeles global orzular sanoatining markaziga aylandi.

Hollywood va global madaniyat

Bugungi kunda biz global madaniyatga ega dunyoda yashayapmiz. Hollywood bu madaniyatni yaratmadi, ammo u uning katta qismiga aylandi. Bizga yoqadimi yoki yo'qmi, Hollywood filmlari butun dunyo bo'ylab odamlarning orzu qilish va o'ylash tarzini o'zgartirdi.

Fakt: Paramountning tog' logotipida dastlab 24 ta yulduz bor edi, bu studiya bilan birinchi shartnoma imzolagan 24 ta aktyorni ifodalaydi. Keyinchalik, u 22 ta yulduzga o'zgartirildi, ammo aniq sababi sirligicha qolmoqda!

Birinchi velosiped

Kovid va kelayotgan iqlim inqirozi bilan velosipedlar tobora ommalashib bormoqda. Ammo velosiped qayerdan kelgan? Bu "velosiped"ni kim ixtiro qilgan?

Velosiped hikoyasi

Velosiped temir yo'l lokomotivdan keyin ixtiro qilinganini bilarmidingiz?

Bu ajablanarli bo'lishi mumkin, ammo ikki g'ildirakli transport vositasi 1839 yilda shotlandiyalik temirchi Kirkpatrick MacMillan tomonidan birinchi marta yasalganidan beri uzoq yo'lni bosib o'tdi.

Birinchi velosiped

MacMillan o'z g'oyasini "xobbi ot" deb nomlangan eski yog'och transport vositasidan olgan. Bu transport vositasining ikkita g'ildiragi bor edi va haydovchi oldinga siljish uchun oyoqlari bilan itarishi kerak edi. Biroq, u juda xavfsiz emas edi, chunki unda rul va tormoz yo'q edi.

MacMillan, kim "Telba Pate" deb nomlangan, ba'zi muhim o'zgarishlar kiritdi. U:

  • Harakatlanuvchi panjalar tizimini qo'shdi – haydovchi orqa g'ildirakni aylantirish uchun panjalarni oyoqlari bilan itarishi mumkin edi.
  • Old g'ildirakni aylantirdi – endi velosipedni boshqarish mumkin edi.

O'zining yangi velosipedining qanday ishlashini ko'rsatish uchun MacMillan Glazgoga 60 mil yo'l bosib bordi! Bu juda qiyin sayohat edi, chunki yo'llar yomon edi va velosipedda rezina shinalar yoki amortizatorlar yo'q edi.

Velosipedlar qanday yaxshilandi

MacMillan o'z ixtirosi bilan mashhur bo'lmadi, ammo boshqa odamlarga velosiped g'oyasi yoqdi.

25 yil o'tgach, Per Lallemant ismli fransuz pedalli birinchi velosipedni loyihalashtirdi.

1876 yilda H.J. Lawson zanjirli uzatmani qo'shdi, bu velosipedlarni minishni ancha osonlashtirdi.

Keyinchalik, rezina shinalar va uzatmalar qo'shildi, ammo velosipedning asosiy dizayni o'shandan beri ko'p o'zgarmadi.

Fakt: Tadqiqotlar shuni ko'rsatadiki, muntazam velosiped haydash umringizga 3-5 yil qo'shishi mumkin! Shuningdek, u yuragingizni sog'lom tutadi va stressni kamaytiradi.

Bugungi velosiped

Bugungi kunda velosiped dunyodagi eng mashhur transport turlaridan biri, ayniqsa rivojlanayotgan mamlakatlarda. U arzon, ifloslantirmaydi va foydalanish oson.

Kelajakda velosipedlar yirik shaharlarda sayohat qilishning eng yaxshi usuli bo'lishi mumkin. Rahmat, Pate!
