The origins of Football and Rugby

Games that England gave the world

Football and rugby began their lives in England, hundreds of years ago. In those days, there were different ball games in different places; and even today, there are some places in England where people still play ancient forms of the game. Early football had very few rules, and the rules changed from one place to another. The basic rule was simple; a team tried to move a ball past the other team's line. Sometimes the lines were a long way apart.

In the age of Queen Victoria, public schools began to use standard rules. In some schools, players were not allowed to carry the ball; in other schools, like Rugby College, they were allowed to carry it. So two different games emerged; on the one hand football, on the other hand, "rugby football".

In 1863, some men formed the Football Association in London, and fixed the rules.

Eighteen years later, the first F.A. Cup took place. Football was very popular with workers in the north of England, particularly in industrial cities like Manchester and Liverpool. By 1888, the Football League was established with 12 clubs.

Today, football is the most popular ball game in the world, and rugby is a very international sport too.

Fact: More than 4 billion people watch or play football, making it the most popular sport globally!

Futbol va Regbi kelib chiqishi

Angliya dunyoga bergan o'yinlar

Futbol va regbi Angliyada, yuzlab yillar avval paydo bo'lgan. O'sha kunlarda turli joylarda turli xil to'p o'yinlari bor edi; va hatto bugungi kunda ham, Angliyada odamlar hali ham o'yinning qadimiy shakllarini o'ynaydigan joylar bor. Dastlabki futbol juda kam qoidalarga ega edi, va qoidalar bir joydan ikkinchisiga o'zgarib turardi. Asosiy qoida oddiy edi; jamoa to'pni boshqa jamoaning chizig'idan o'tkazishga harakat qilardi. Ba'zan chiziqlar uzoq masofada joylashgan edi.

Qirolicha Viktoriya davrida, jamoat maktablari standart qoidalardan foydalana boshladi. Ba'zi maktablarda o'yinchilarga to'pni olib yurishga ruxsat berilmagan; boshqa maktablarda, masalan, Regbi kollejida, ularga to'pni olib yurishga ruxsat berilgan. Shunday qilib, ikki xil o'yin paydo bo'ldi; bir tomondan futbol, boshqa tomondan, "regbi futboli".

1863-yilda ba'zi erkaklar Londonda Futbol Assotsiatsiyasini tuzdilar va qoidalarni belgilashdi.

O'n sakkiz yil o'tib, birinchi F.A. Kubogi bo'lib o'tdi. Futbol Angliyaning shimolidagi ishchilar orasida, ayniqsa Manchester va Liverpul kabi sanoat shaharlarida juda mashhur edi. 1888-yilga kelib, Futbol Ligasi 12 ta klub bilan tashkil etildi.

Bugungi kunda futbol dunyodagi eng mashhur to'p o'yini, va regbi ham juda xalqaro sport turi.
