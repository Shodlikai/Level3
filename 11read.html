City life is cool; but is country life cooler?

Apparently yes. After the terrible Covid-19 epidemic of 2020, lots of people would like to leave their city and live in the country. More and more people in Britain want to live in the country, and this is causing more and more problems in some rural areas.

The population of British cities has been falling for years. Cities like Liverpool and Glasgow have lost about 30% of their population in 30 years. But Britain’s population is still growing. Where are the people going?

Answer: to the country.

The English countryside has a classic image. People imagine that life in the country is slow and calm; that there are no traffic jams, no pollution, and no crime. In some places, this is true; but in others it is not.

Small towns and villages are becoming more and more popular; people have more space and most houses have gardens. But problems are growing. Lots of people want to live in the country and work in the city; so more and more people travel long distances each day to go to work. Of course they don’t use buses (they are too slow) or trains (they don’t stop in the country); they use cars. And although they live in the country, they want to use supermarkets and good fast roads…. but they do not want to see them or hear them. Besides, lots of young people say that life in the country is boring: there is not enough to do, there are not enough activities and excitement.

Little villages now have traffic problems in the morning, just like big cities! And they are getting worse.

The problems are very complicated! If everyone moves into the country, large parts of the countryside will disappear! People leave cities and big towns to escape from urban problems; but more and more, they are bringing their problems with them.

On warm summer days, and cold winter days, air pollution can be a big problem in large parts of the south of England, not just in London. Traffic jams are now often part of life, even in the country; crime has become a serious problem in rural areas.

Perhaps there is hope for the future. Soon Britain’s population will stop rising. From about the year 2030, it will perhaps start to fall. In 100 year’s time, there will be less people in Britain than today - perhaps two million less. No doubt there will be less pollution too; oil and petrol will probably be rare by then.

Fact: Studies show that people in countryside areas often live longer because they have less stress, more physical activity, and cleaner air.

A Short History Of Spacecraft Landings

On The Moon

A block of birds fly in front of the full moon over the city center in Tallinn, Estonia, Oct. 17, 2024.

A Short History of Moon Landings ●✓

Many years have passed since the first spacecraft landed on the moon in 1966. Since then, different countries have tried to land on the moon. Some missions succeeded, while others failed. Here is a short history of some important moon landings.

First Victories

The Soviet Union’s Luna 9 was the first spacecraft to land on the moon. It touched the surface in 1966 after many failed attempts. NASA said Luna 9 was the first to carry out a soft landing and to send photos of the moon’s surface to Earth. The landing also proved that the moon’s surface was solid and could support a spacecraft.

In May 1966, the United States followed with Surveyor 1. NASA called it the first of seven robotic spacecraft sent to the moon to collect data for the Apollo missions. The soft landing of Surveyor 1 was one of NASA’s great early successes.

Both the U.S. and the Soviet Union had more successful robotic landings. After that, they focused on sending humans to The moon.

In 1969, NASA’s Apollo 11 successfully landed Neil Armstrong and Buzz Aldrin on the moon. The Apollo program sent 12 astronauts to explore the moon across six missions. The last mission, Apollo 17, was in 1972.

The U.S. is still the only country to have landed humans on the moon. NASA’s Artemis program hopes to return astronauts to the lunar surface by 2026.

China Expands Space Efforts

In 2013, China became the third country to land a spacecraft on the moon. It sent a rover, Yutu, to explore the surface.

China continued its space success in 2019 when the Yutu-2 rover landed on the far side of the moon—a place no spacecraft had reached before. In 2020, China successfully returned moon samples to Earth, collecting 1.7 kilograms of rock and soil. In 2024, another mission returned samples from the far side of the moon. China has said it aims to send astronauts to the moon by 2030.

Russia’s Landing Failure

In 2023, Russia attempted its first moon landing in nearly 50 years with Luna 25. The spacecraft was supposed to land near the lunar south pole, but it crashed.

The country’s last successful mission was Luna 24 in 1976, which brought moon rocks back to Earth.

India Succeeds on Second Attempt

India’s first lander crashed in 2019, but in 2023, it succeeded on its second attempt. The Chandrayaan-3 spacecraft landed safely, making India the fourth nation to complete a moon landing.

Japan Lands Sideways

In January 2024, Japan became the fifth country to land a spacecraft on the moon. However, the SLIM spacecraft landed sideways, causing power and communication problems. Even though the lander was not in the right position, it was able to operate for a short time.

The Future of Moon Exploration

Today, many countries are planning new moon missions. NASA, China, and other space agencies hope to send astronauts back to the moon soon.

Maybe one day, humans will live on the moon!

Fact:

NASA (National Aeronautics and Space Administration) was founded on July 29, 1958 by the U.S. government. It replaced an earlier space organization called NACA (National Advisory Committee for Aeronautics).

Shahar hayoti ajoyib; lekin qishloq hayoti undan ham ajoyibroqmi?

Shubhasiz, ha. 2020-yildagi dahshatli Covid-19 epidemiyasidan so'ng, ko'p odamlar shaharlarini tark etib, qishloqda yashashni xohlashadi. Britaniyada tobora ko'proq odamlar qishloqda yashashni xohlayapti, bu esa ba'zi qishloq joylarida ko'proq muammolarga sabab bo'lmoqda.

Britaniya shaharlarining aholisi yillar davomida kamayib bormoqda. Liverpul va Glazgo kabi shaharlar 30 yil ichida o'z aholisining taxminan 30 foizini yo'qotdi. Ammo Britaniya aholisi hali ham o'sib bormoqda. Odamlar qayerga ketmoqda?

Javob: qishloqqa.

Angliya qishloqlari klassik tasavvurga ega. Odamlar qishloqdagi hayot sekin va sokin deb o'ylashadi; u yerda tirbandliklar, ifloslanish va jinoyat yo'q. Ba'zi joylarda bu haqiqat; lekin boshqa joylarda emas.

Kichik shaharlar va qishloqlar tobora ommalashib bormoqda; odamlar ko'proq joyga ega va aksariyat uylarning bog'lari bor. Ammo muammolar o'sib bormoqda. Ko'p odamlar qishloqda yashashni va shaharda ishlashni xohlashadi; shuning uchun tobora ko'proq odamlar har kuni ishga borish uchun uzoq masofalarga sayohat qilishadi. Albatta, ular avtobuslardan (ular juda sekin) yoki poyezdlardan (ular qishloqda to'xtamaydi) foydalanmaydi; ular mashinadan foydalanishadi. Va garchi ular qishloqda yashasa-da, ular supermarketlar va yaxshi tezkor yo'llardan foydalanishni xohlashadi... lekin ularni ko'rishni yoki eshitishni xohlashmaydi. Bundan tashqari, ko'plab yoshlar qishloqdagi hayotni zerikarli deb aytishadi: qiladigan ishlar, tadbirlar va hayajon yetarli emas.

Endi kichik qishloqlarda ham ertalab tirbandlik muammolari bor, xuddi katta shaharlar kabi! Va ular yomonlashib bormoqda.

Muammolar juda murakkab! Agar hamma qishloqqa ko'chib o'tsa, qishloq joylarining katta qismi yo'q bo'lib ketadi! Odamlar shahar muammolaridan qochish uchun shaharlar va katta shaharlarni tark etishadi; lekin tobora ko'proq ular muammolarini o'zlari bilan olib kelishmoqda.

Issiq yoz kunlarida va sovuq qish kunlarida havo ifloslanishi Angliyaning janubidagi katta qismlarida, nafaqat Londonda, katta muammo bo'lishi mumkin. Tirbandliklar endi hatto qishloqda ham hayotning bir qismiga aylanib bormoqda; jinoyat qishloq joylarida jiddiy muammoga aylandi.

Balki kelajak uchun umid bordir. Tez orada Britaniya aholisi o'sishdan to'xtaydi. Taxminan 2030-yildan boshlab, u kamayishni boshlaydi. 100 yildan so'ng, Britaniyada bugungidan kamroq odamlar bo'ladi - ehtimol ikki millionga kamroq. Shubhasiz, ifloslanish ham kamayadi; neft va benzin o'shanda kamyob bo'lib qoladi.

Fakt: Tadqiqotlar shuni ko'rsatadiki, qishloq joylaridagi odamlar kamroq stress, ko'proq jismoniy faollik va toza havo tufayli ko'pincha uzoqroq umr ko'rishadi.

Oydagi kosmik kemalar qo'nishining qisqacha tarixi

2024-yil 17-oktabr, Tallin, Estoniya. Bir guruh qushlar to'lin oy oldidan shahar markazi ustidan uchib o'tmoqda.

Oydagi qo'nishlarning qisqacha tarixi ●✓

1966-yilda birinchi kosmik kema oyga qo'nganidan beri ko'p yillar o'tdi. Shundan beri turli mamlakatlar oyga qo'nishga harakat qilishdi. Ba'zi missiyalar muvaffaqiyatli yakunlandi, ba'zilari esa muvaffaqiyatsizlikka uchradi. Bu yerda ba'zi muhim oy qo'nishlarining qisqacha tarixi berilgan.

Birinchi G'alabalar

Sovet Ittifoqining "Luna 9" kosmik kemasi oyga qo'ngan birinchi kosmik kema bo'ldi. U ko'p muvaffaqiyatsiz urinishlardan so'ng 1966-yilda oy yuzasiga yetib bordi. NASAning aytishicha, "Luna 9" yumshoq qo'nishni amalga oshirgan va oy yuzasining fotosuratlarini Yerga yuborgan birinchi kema bo'lgan. Qo'nish, shuningdek, oy yuzasi qattiq ekanligini va kosmik kemani ko'tara olishini isbotladi.

1966-yil may oyida Qo'shma Shtatlar "Surveyor 1" bilan unga ergashdi. NASA uni "Apollon" missiyalari uchun ma'lumot to'plash maqsadida oyga yuborilgan yetti robotli kosmik kemaning birinchisi deb atadi. "Surveyor 1" ning yumshoq qo'nishi NASAning dastlabki katta muvaffaqiyatlaridan biri bo'ldi.

AQSh va Sovet Ittifoqi ham ko'proq muvaffaqiyatli robotli qo'nishlarni amalga oshirdi. Shundan so'ng, ular odamlarni Oyga yuborishga e'tibor qaratishdi.

1969-yilda NASAning "Apollon 11" missiyasi Nil Armstrong va Bazz Oldrinni oyga muvaffaqiyatli qo'ndirdi. "Apollon" dasturi oltita missiya davomida oyni o'rganish uchun 12 nafar astronavtni yubordi. Oxirgi missiya - "Apollon 17" 1972-yilda bo'lgan.

AQSh hali ham oyga odam qo'ndirgan yagona davlatdir. NASAning "Artemis" dasturi 2026-yilgacha astronavtlarni oy yuzasiga qaytarishga umid qilmoqda.

Xitoyning Kengayib Borayotgan Kosmik Harakatlari

2013-yilda Xitoy oyga kosmik kema qo'ndirgan uchinchi davlatga aylandi. U oy yuzasini o'rganish uchun "Yutu" nomli roverni yubordi.

Xitoy 2019-yilda "Yutu-2" roveri oyning narigi tomoniga qo'nganida kosmik muvaffaqiyatini davom ettirdi – bu joyga ilgari hech qanday kosmik kema yetib bormagan edi. 2020-yilda Xitoy 1.7 kilogramm tosh va tuproq yig'ib, oy namunalarini Yerga muvaffaqiyatli qaytardi. 2024-yilda yana bir missiya oyning narigi tomonidan namunalar qaytardi. Xitoy 2030-yilgacha oyga astronavtlarni yuborishni maqsad qilganini aytdi.

Rossiyaning Muvaffaqiyatsiz Qo'nishi

2023-yilda Rossiya 50 yil ichida birinchi marta "Luna 25" bilan oyga qo'nishga urindi. Kosmik kema oyning janubiy qutbi yaqiniga qo'nishi kerak edi, ammo u qulab tushdi.

Mamlakatning oxirgi muvaffaqiyatli missiyasi 1976-yildagi "Luna 24" bo'lib, u oy toshlarini Yerga qaytargan edi.

Hindiston Ikkinchi Urinishda Muvaffaqiyatga Erishdi

Hindistonning birinchi landeri 2019-yilda qulab tushdi, ammo 2023-yilda ikkinchi urinishida muvaffaqiyatga erishdi. "Chandrayaan-3" kosmik kemasi xavfsiz qo'ndi va Hindistonni oyga qo'nishni yakunlagan to'rtinchi davlatga aylantirdi.

Yaponiya Yonboshiga Qo'ndi

2024-yil yanvar oyida Yaponiya oyga kosmik kema qo'ndirgan beshinchi davlatga aylandi. Biroq, "SLIM" kosmik kemasi yonboshiga qo'nib, quvvat va aloqa muammolariga sabab bo'ldi. Lander to'g'ri holatda bo'lmasa-da, u qisqa vaqt davomida ishlay oldi.

Oyni O'rganishning Kelajagi

Bugungi kunda ko'plab mamlakatlar yangi oy missiyalarini rejalashtirmoqda. NASA, Xitoy va boshqa kosmik agentliklar tez orada astronavtlarni oyga qaytarishga umid qilmoqda.
