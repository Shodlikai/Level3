Majburiyat, zarurat, maslahat va boshqa holatlarni ifodalash

Asosiy Qoidalar

Must

Must modal fe'li kuchli majburiyat, zarurat yoki biror narsaning to'g'riligiga ishonchni ifodalash uchun ishlatiladi. Bu ko'pincha shaxsiy majburiyat yoki qoida/qonunni bildiradi.

  • Majburiyat/Zarurat (shaxsiy yoki ichki): Biror narsa qilish kerakligi yoki zarurati.
  • Kuchli tavsiya: Biror narsa qilish juda muhimligi.
  • Xulosa/Ishonch: Biror narsaning haqiqat ekanligiga kuchli ishonch.

You must wear a seatbelt in the car. (Mashinada xavfsizlik kamarini taqishingiz shart.) - Qoida/Qonun

I must finish this report by Friday. (Men bu hisobotni juma kungacha tugatishim shart.) - Shaxsiy majburiyat

She must be tired after such a long journey. (U shuncha uzoq safardan keyin charchagan bo'lishi kerak.) - Kuchli ishonch

Eslatma: Must not (mustn't) biror narsani qilish taqiqlanganligini yoki mutlaqo mumkin emasligini bildiradi.

You mustn't smoke here. (Bu yerda chekish mumkin emas.)

Have to / Had to

Have to modal fe'li tashqi majburiyat, qoidalar yoki vaziyatlar tufayli biror narsani qilish zaruratini ifodalaydi. Bu ko'pincha sub'ektning xohishidan tashqari bo'lgan majburiyatni bildiradi.

  • Tashqi majburiyat/Zarurat: Qoidalar, qonunlar yoki boshqa odamlar tomonidan qo'yilgan majburiyat.

I have to work tomorrow. (Men ertaga ishlashim kerak.) - Ish majburiyati

Students have to wear uniforms. (Talabalar forma kiyishlari shart.) - Qoida

Had to (O'tgan zamon)

Had to fe'li have to ning o'tgan zamon shakli bo'lib, o'tgan zamondagi majburiyat yoki zaruratni ifodalaydi.

We had to leave early yesterday. (Biz kecha erta ketishimiz kerak edi.)

She had to study hard for the exam. (U imtihon uchun qattiq o'qishi kerak edi.)

Don't have to / Doesn't have to

Don't have to (I/You/We/They uchun) va Doesn't have to (He/She/It uchun) biror narsani qilish zaruriyati yo'qligini, ya'ni majburiyat yo'qligini bildiradi. Bu biror narsa taqiqlanganligini emas, balki shunchaki shart emasligini anglatadi.

You don't have to come if you don't want to. (Agar xohlamasangiz, kelishingiz shart emas.)

He doesn't have to work on Sundays. (U yakshanba kunlari ishlashga majbur emas.)

We didn't have to pay for the tickets. (Biz chiptalar uchun pul to'lashimiz shart emas edi.) - O'tgan zamon

Should / Ought to

Should va Ought to modal fe'llari maslahat, tavsiya, kutish yoki majburiyatning yumshoqroq shaklini ifodalash uchun ishlatiladi.

Should

  • Maslahat/Tavsiya: Nima qilish to'g'ri yoki yaxshi ekanligini aytish.
  • Kutish: Biror narsaning yuz berishi kerakligini kutish.
  • Majburiyat (yumshoq): Biror narsani qilish kerakligini, lekin majburiy emasligini bildirish.

You should study more for your exams. (Imtihonlaringizga ko'proq o'qishingiz kerak.) - Maslahat

It's late, they should be home by now. (Kech bo'ldi, ular hozir uyda bo'lishlari kerak.) - Kutish

Ought to

Ought to fe'li ma'no jihatidan should ga juda o'xshash va ko'pincha uning o'rniga ishlatilishi mumkin. Bu ko'proq rasmiyroq yoki axloqiy majburiyatni bildiradi.

You ought to apologize for your behavior. (Xulq-atvoringiz uchun uzr so'rashingiz kerak.) - Axloqiy majburiyat

We ought to help those in need. (Biz muhtojlarga yordam berishimiz kerak.)

Had better

Had better modal fe'li kuchli maslahat yoki ogohlantirishni ifodalash uchun ishlatiladi. Agar tavsiyaga amal qilinmasa, salbiy oqibatlar bo'lishi mumkinligini bildiradi. U qisqa shaklda 'd better deb ham ishlatiladi.

  • Kuchli maslahat/Ogohlantirish: Biror narsa qilish zarur, aks holda muammo paydo bo'lishi mumkin.

You had better hurry, or you'll miss the train. (Tezroq shoshiling, aks holda poyezdga kechikib qolasiz.)

We'd better leave now. (Biz hozir ketganimiz ma'qul.)

Eslatma: Had better odatda so'roq gaplarda ishlatilmaydi. Buning o'rniga should yoki do you think...? kabi iboralar ishlatiladi.

Noto'g'ri: Had I better go?

To'g'ri: Should I go? yoki Do you think I had better go?

Modal Fe'llar orasidagi farqlar

Must vs Have to

Ikkala fe'l ham majburiyatni bildiradi, lekin ularning kelib chiqishi farqlanadi.

Fe'lMa'nosiKelib chiqishiMisol
Must Ichki majburiyat, shaxsiy zarurat, kuchli tavsiya, ishonch. Gapiruvchining o'z his-tuyg'ulari, fikri yoki shaxsiy qoidasi. I must call my mother. (O'zimni majbur his qilyapman.)
Have to Tashqi majburiyat, qoidalar, qonunlar, vaziyatlar. Tashqi kuchlar, qoidalar, boshqa odamlar. I have to wear a uniform at work. (Ish joyidagi qoida.)

Eslatma: O'tgan zamonda faqat had to ishlatiladi, must ning o'tgan zamon shakli yo'q.

I had to study for the exam. (Men imtihonga o'qishim kerak edi.)

Should vs Ought to

Bu ikkala fe'l ham maslahat yoki tavsiyani bildiradi va ko'pincha bir-birining o'rniga ishlatilishi mumkin.

Fe'lMa'nosiIshlatilishiMisol
Should Umumiy maslahat, tavsiya, kutish. Ko'proq keng tarqalgan va kundalik nutqda ishlatiladi. You should see a doctor. (Shifokorga ko'rinishingiz kerak.)
Ought to Kuchliroq maslahat, axloqiy majburiyat, to'g'ri ishni qilish. Ko'proq rasmiyroq va ba'zan "should" ga nisbatan kuchliroq ma'no beradi. You ought to respect your elders. (Kattalarni hurmat qilishingiz kerak.)

Modal Fe'llar Strukturalari

Must (Majburiyat)

Ega + must + V (asosiy shakli)

1. I must finish this work today.

Men bu ishni bugun tugatishim shart.

2. You must follow the rules.

Siz qoidalarga rioya qilishingiz shart.

3. She must be here by 5 PM.

U soat 5 gacha shu yerda bo'lishi kerak.

Mustn't (Taqiq)

Ega + mustn't + V (asosiy shakli)

1. You mustn't park here.

Bu yerga mashina qo'yishingiz mumkin emas.

2. We mustn't be late.

Biz kechikmasligimiz kerak.

3. He mustn't tell anyone.

U hech kimga aytmasligi kerak.

Have to / Has to (Majburiyat)

Ega + have to / has to + V (asosiy shakli)

1. I have to go now.

Men hozir ketishim kerak.

2. She has to work on weekends.

U dam olish kunlari ishlashi kerak.

3. They have to study for the test.

Ular testga o'qishlari kerak.

Had to (O'tgan zamon majburiyati)

Ega + had to + V (asosiy shakli)

1. We had to cancel the trip.

Biz sayohatni bekor qilishimiz kerak edi.

2. He had to apologize.

U uzr so'rashi kerak edi.

3. I had to wait for an hour.

Men bir soat kutishimga to'g'ri keldi.

Don't / Doesn't have to (Majburiyat yo'qligi)

Ega + don't / doesn't have to + V (asosiy shakli)

1. You don't have to pay.

Siz pul to'lashingiz shart emas.

2. She doesn't have to come.

U kelishi shart emas.

3. We don't have to rush.

Biz shoshilishimiz shart emas.

Should / Ought to (Maslahat)

Ega + should / ought to + V (asosiy shakli)

1. You should listen to your parents.

Siz ota-onangizni tinglashingiz kerak.

2. He ought to apologize.

U uzr so'rashi kerak.

3. We should save money.

Biz pul tejashimiz kerak.

Had better (Kuchli maslahat/Ogohlantirish)

Ega + had better + V (asosiy shakli)

1. You had better study.

Yaxshisi, o'qishingiz kerak.

2. We'd better leave now.

Yaxshisi, hozir ketganimiz ma'qul.

3. She had better not be late.

Yaxshisi, u kechikmasligi kerak.

Should / Ought to + Have + V3 (O'tgan zamon)

Ega + should / ought to + have + V3

1. I should have studied harder.

Men qattiqroq o'qishim kerak edi (lekin o'qimadim).

2. They ought to have arrived by now.

Ular hozirgacha yetib kelishlari kerak edi (lekin kelishmadi).

3. You shouldn't have told her.

Siz unga aytmasligingiz kerak edi (lekin aytdingiz).

Should / Ought to + Be + V(ing) (Davomli)

Ega + should / ought to + be + V(ing)

1. You should be working now.

Siz hozir ishlashingiz kerak.

2. He ought to be studying.

U o'qiyotgan bo'lishi kerak.

3. They shouldn't be playing so loudly.

Ular bunchalik shovqin qilmasliklari kerak.

Modal Fe'llar Misollari

Majburiyat va Zarurat

You must submit the report by Friday.

Hisobotni juma kungacha topshirishingiz shart.

I have to wake up early tomorrow for work.

Ertaga ishga erta turishim kerak.

She had to leave the party early.

U kechani erta tark etishi kerak edi.

You mustn't talk during the exam.

Imtihon paytida gaplashishingiz mumkin emas.

Maslahat va Tavsiya

You should eat more vegetables.

Ko'proq sabzavot yeyishingiz kerak.

We ought to help our neighbors.

Biz qo'shnilarimizga yordam berishimiz kerak.

You had better finish your homework before going out.

Tashqariga chiqishdan oldin uy vazifangizni tugatganingiz ma'qul.

He shouldn't ignore the warning signs.

U ogohlantirish belgilarini e'tiborsiz qoldirmasligi kerak.

Zarurat yo'qligi va O'tgan zamon

You don't have to wear a tie.

Galstuk taqishingiz shart emas.

She doesn't have to work on holidays.

U bayram kunlari ishlashga majbur emas.

I should have called her yesterday.

Men unga kecha qo'ng'iroq qilishim kerak edi (lekin qilmadim).

They ought to have told us the truth.

Ular bizga haqiqatni aytishlari kerak edi (lekin aytishmadi).

Davomli harakat va Taxmin

You should be studying for your exam right now.

Siz hozir imtihoningizga o'qishingiz kerak.

He ought to be sleeping at this hour.

U bu soatda uxlayotgan bo'lishi kerak.

She must be feeling much better now.

U hozir ancha yaxshi his qilayotgan bo'lishi kerak.

They shouldn't be making so much noise.

Ular bunchalik shovqin qilmasliklari kerak.

2. It is forbidden to smoke here. (Use: mustn't)

3. I advise you to see a doctor. (Use: should)

4. It would be a good idea for us to leave now. (Use: had better)
