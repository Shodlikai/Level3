Adverbs

Ravishlar (Adverbs) haqida to'liq ma'lumot, turlari, tuzilishi, ishlatilishi va ko'plab mashqlar bilan mukammal o'rganing.

Asosiy Qoidalar

Ravish (Adverb) nima?

Ravish (Adverb) fe'l (verb), sifat (adjective) yoki boshqa bir ravish (adverb) haqida qo'shimcha ma'lumot beruvchi so'z turkumidir. Ular qanday, qayerda, qachon, qanchalik tez-tez yoki qanchalik darajada sodir bo'lganligini bildiradi.

Masalan: He sings beautifully. (Qanday kuylaydi? Chiroyli qilib.)

She ran very quickly. (Qanchalik tez yugurdi? Juda tez.)

Ravishlarning turlari

  • Harakat ravishlari (Adverbs of Manner): Harakat qanday bajarilganini bildiradi. Ko'pincha sifatga -ly qo'shimchasi qo'shish orqali yasaladi.

    Misollar: slowly (sekin), carefully (ehtiyotkorlik bilan), happily (xursand holda), well (yaxshi).

  • Joy ravishlari (Adverbs of Place): Harakat qayerda sodir bo'lganini bildiradi.

    Misollar: here (bu yerda), there (u yerda), everywhere (hamma joyda), upstairs (yuqorida), inside (ichkarida).

  • Vaqt ravishlari (Adverbs of Time): Harakat qachon sodir bo'lganini bildiradi.

    Misollar: now (hozir), yesterday (kecha), soon (tez orada), later (keyinroq), today (bugun).

  • Chastota ravishlari (Adverbs of Frequency): Harakat qanchalik tez-tez sodir bo'lganini bildiradi.

    Misollar: always (har doim), often (tez-tez), sometimes (ba'zan), never (hech qachon), usually (odatda).

  • Daraja ravishlari (Adverbs of Degree): Sifat yoki boshqa ravishning darajasini, kuchini bildiradi.

    Misollar: very (juda), too (juda ham), extremely (o'ta), almost (deyarli), quite (ancha).

  • So'roq ravishlari (Interrogative Adverbs): Savol berish uchun ishlatiladi (qayerda, qachon, qanday, nima uchun).

    Misollar: where, when, how, why.

  • Nisbiy ravishlar (Relative Adverbs): Ikki gapni bog'lash uchun ishlatiladi (joy, vaqt, sabab).

    Misollar: where, when, why.

Ravishlarning yasalishi

Ko'pchilik ravishlar sifatlarga -ly qo'shimchasini qo'shish orqali yasaladi:

  • quick (sifat) → quickly (ravish)
  • careful (sifat) → carefully (ravish)
  • happy (sifat) → happily (ravish) (y harfi i ga o'zgaradi)
  • terrible (sifat) → terribly (ravish) (-le bilan tugasa e tushib qoladi)

Istisnolar va tartibsiz ravishlar:

  • good (sifat) → well (ravish)
  • fast (sifat) → fast (ravish) (o'zgarmaydi)
  • hard (sifat) → hard (ravish) (o'zgarmaydi)
  • late (sifat) → late (ravish) (o'zgarmaydi)

Ravishlarning darajalari (Positive, Comparative, Superlative Adverbs)

Ravishlar ham sifatlar kabi uchta darajaga ega bo'lishi mumkin: oddiy (Positive), qiyosiy (Comparative) va orttirma (Superlative).

1. Oddiy daraja (Positive Degree)

Ravishning asosiy shakli bo'lib, hech qanday taqqoslashni bildirmaydi.

Misollar: quickly, slowly, well, fast.

2. Qiyosiy daraja (Comparative Degree)

Ikki harakat yoki holatni taqqoslash uchun ishlatiladi. Odatda than bilan birga keladi.

  • Bir bo'g'inli ravishlar yoki sifat bilan bir xil bo'lganlar: Ravishga -er qo'shimchasi qo'shiladi.

    Misollar: fast → faster (tezroq), hard → harder (jiddiyroq), early → earlier (ertaroq).

    Gapda: He runs faster than me. (U mendan tezroq yuguradi.)

  • Ikki yoki undan ortiq bo'g'inli ravishlar (odatda -ly bilan tugaydiganlar): Ravishdan oldin more so'zi ishlatiladi.

    Misollar: carefully → more carefully (ehtiyotkorroq), beautifully → more beautifully (chiroyliroq).

    Gapda: She sings more beautifully than her sister. (U opasidan chiroyliroq kuylaydi.)

  • Tartibsiz ravishlar:

    well → better (yaxshiroq)

    badly → worse (yomonroq)

    little → less (kamroq)

    much → more (ko'proq)

    Gapda: He drives better now. (U hozir yaxshiroq haydaydi.)

3. Orttirma daraja (Superlative Degree)

Uch yoki undan ortiq harakat/holat orasidagi eng yuqori darajani bildiradi. Odatda the artikli bilan birga keladi (lekin ravishlarda ba'zan tushib qolishi mumkin).

  • Bir bo'g'inli ravishlar yoki sifat bilan bir xil bo'lganlar: Ravishga -est qo'shimchasi qo'shiladi.

    Misollar: fast → fastest (eng tez), hard → hardest (eng jiddiy), early → earliest (eng erta).

    Gapda: He runs the fastest of all. (U hammadan eng tez yuguradi.)

  • Ikki yoki undan ortiq bo'g'inli ravishlar (odatda -ly bilan tugaydiganlar): Ravishdan oldin most so'zi ishlatiladi.

    Misollar: carefully → most carefully (eng ehtiyotkor), beautifully → most beautifully (eng chiroyli).

    Gapda: She sings most beautifully in the choir. (U xorda eng chiroyli kuylaydi.)

  • Tartibsiz ravishlar:

    well → best (eng yaxshi)

    badly → worst (eng yomon)

    little → least (eng kam)

    much → most (eng ko'p)

    Gapda: He performed best in the exam. (U imtihonda eng yaxshi natija ko'rsatdi.)

as + Positive Adverb + as qoidasi

Bu struktura ikki harakat yoki holatni teng darajada ekanligini taqqoslash uchun ishlatiladi. Ravish har doim oddiy (positive) darajada bo'ladi.

Ega + Fe'l + as + Oddiy ravish + as + Ot/Olmosh

Misollar:

  • He runs as fast as a cheetah. (U gepard kabi tez yuguradi.)
  • She sings as beautifully as a professional. (U professional kabi chiroyli kuylaydi.)
  • I can type as quickly as you. (Men siz kabi tez yozishim mumkin.)
  • They arrived as early as possible. (Ular iloji boricha erta yetib kelishdi.)

Ravishlar orasidagi farqlar va chalkashliklar

Ravish (Adverb) vs. Sifat (Adjective)

Bu ikkisi tez-tez chalkashadi. Asosiy farq shundaki:

  • Sifatlar otlarni (nouns) ta'riflaydi.
  • Ravishlar fe'llarni (verbs), sifatlarni (adjectives) yoki boshqa ravishlarni (adverbs) ta'riflaydi.

He runs quickly. (Ravish)
Yasalishi Odatda o'zgarmas shakl Ko'pincha sifatga -ly qo'shish orqali happy (sifat) vs. happily (ravish)

Eslatma: Ba'zi so'zlar (fast, hard, late, early) ham sifat, ham ravish bo'lib kelishi mumkin va shakli o'zgarmaydi.

Shakli bir xil bo'lgan sifat va ravishlar

Ba'zi so'zlar ham sifat, ham ravish sifatida ishlatilishi mumkin va ularning shakli o'zgarmaydi. Ularning gapdagi vazifasini aniqlash uchun kontekstga e'tibor berish kerak.

  • Fast:

    Sifat: He is a fast runner. (U tez yuguruvchi.)

    Ravish: He runs fast. (U tez yuguradi.)

  • Hard:

    Sifat: This is a hard test. (Bu qiyin test.)

    Ravish: She works hard. (U jiddiy ishlaydi.)

  • Late:

    Sifat: It was a late decision. (Bu kech qabul qilingan qaror edi.)

    Ravish: He arrived late. (U kech keldi.)

  • Early:

    Sifat: It was an early morning. (Bu erta tong edi.)

    Ravish: She woke up early. (U erta uyg'ondi.)

-ly qo'shimchasi bilan ma'nosi o'zgaradigan ravishlar

Ba'zi sifatlarga -ly qo'shilganda ular ravishga aylanadi, lekin ba'zan bu qo'shimcha so'zning ma'nosini butunlay o'zgartirib yuborishi mumkin.

Ravishlarning gapdagi o'rni va tuzilishi

Fe'lni ta'riflaganda

Ega + Fe'l + Ravish (Harakat)

1. She sings beautifully.

2. He drives carefully.

3. They speak English fluently.

4. The children played happily.

5. He finished the task quickly.

Sifatni ta'riflaganda

Ravish (Daraja) + Sifat + Ot

1. It was an extremely cold day.

2. She is very talented.

3. The movie was quite interesting.

4. He is really good at math.

5. This cake is too sweet.

Boshqa ravishni ta'riflaganda

Ravish (Daraja) + Ravish (Boshqa)

1. He drives too fast.

2. She speaks very softly.

3. They finished the work quite quickly.

4. He understood the lesson almost perfectly.

5. She sings extremely well.

Chastota ravishlarining o'rni

Ega + Chastota ravishi + Asosiy fe'l
Ega + Yordamchi fe'l + Chastota ravishi + Asosiy fe'l

1. I always drink coffee in the morning.

2. She never eats meat.

3. We are often late for meetings.

4. He has rarely seen such a beautiful sunset.

5. Do you usually go to bed early?

Joy va vaqt ravishlarining o'rni

Gapning oxirida (odatda joy, keyin vaqt)

1. We met here yesterday.

2. She will arrive soon.

3. I looked for my keys everywhere.

4. They live downstairs.

5. He went home early.

Gapning boshida joylashuvi

Ravish, + Ega + Fe'l...

1. Suddenly, the lights went out.

2. Fortunately, no one was hurt.

3. Yesterday, I went to the market.

4. Carefully, he opened the old box.

5. Eventually, they found a solution.

Har bir ravish turiga oid ko'proq misollar

Harakat ravishlari (Manner)

She sings beautifully.

U chiroyli kuylaydi.

He drives carefully.

U ehtiyotkorlik bilan haydaydi.

They speak loudly.

Ular baland ovozda gapirishadi.

He solved the problem easily.

U muammoni osonlik bilan hal qildi.

The baby slept soundly.

Chaqaloq qattiq uxlaydi.

She smiled sweetly.

U shirin jilmaydi.

He writes neatly.

U tartibli yozadi.

They walked slowly.

Ular sekin yurishdi.

Joy ravishlari (Place)

Come here.

Bu yerga kel.

My keys are somewhere.

Kalitlarim qayerdadir.

The children are playing outside.

Bolalar tashqarida o'ynashmoqda.

He lives nearby.

U yaqin atrofda yashaydi.

We looked everywhere.

Biz hamma joyni qidirdik.

She went upstairs.

U yuqoriga chiqdi.

The cat is inside.

Mushuk ichkarida.

They traveled abroad.

Ular chet elga sayohat qilishdi.

Vaqt ravishlari (Time)

I will call you later.

Sizga keyinroq qo'ng'iroq qilaman.

She arrived yesterday.

U kecha keldi.

We need to leave now.

Biz hozir ketishimiz kerak.

He will finish soon.

U tez orada tugatadi.

Did you see him today?

Uni bugun ko'rdingizmi?

I have to go early.

Men erta ketishim kerak.

They met recently.

Ular yaqinda uchrashishdi.

She will be back tomorrow.

U ertaga qaytib keladi.

Chastota ravishlari (Frequency)

I always brush my teeth.

Men har doim tishlarimni yuvaman.

She often visits her grandparents.

U tez-tez buvisi va bobosini ziyorat qiladi.

He sometimes plays tennis.

U ba'zan tennis o'ynaydi.

They rarely go to the cinema.

Ular kinoga kamdan-kam borishadi.

We never eat fast food.

Biz hech qachon fast food yemaymiz.

Are you usually busy on weekends?

Dam olish kunlari odatda bandmisiz?

She frequently travels for work.

U ish yuzasidan tez-tez sayohat qiladi.

He seldom complains.

U kamdan-kam shikoyat qiladi.

Daraja ravishlari (Degree)

She is very beautiful.

U juda chiroyli.

He is too tired to work.

U ishlashga juda charchagan.

The water is extremely cold.

Suv o'ta sovuq.

I am quite happy with the result.

Natijadan ancha xursandman.

He almost fell.

U deyarli yiqilib tushdi.

She is absolutely right.

U mutlaqo haq.

The movie was fairly good.

Film ancha yaxshi edi.

He is just finishing his work.

U ishini tugatmoqda.

Qiyosiy va orttirma darajalar

He runs faster than anyone.

U hammadan tezroq yuguradi.

She sings more beautifully than her sister.

U opasidan chiroyliroq kuylaydi.

He works hardest in the team.

U jamoada eng jiddiy ishlaydi.

She arrived earliest of all.

U hammadan eng erta yetib keldi.

This car drives best.

Bu mashina eng yaxshi haydaydi.

He performed worse than expected.

U kutilganidan yomonroq natija ko'rsatdi.

as...as qoidasi

He runs as fast as a cheetah.

U gepard kabi tez yuguradi.

She sings as beautifully as a professional.

U professional kabi chiroyli kuylaydi.

I can type as quickly as you.

Men siz kabi tez yozishim mumkin.

They arrived as early as possible.

Ular iloji boricha erta yetib kelishdi.

He speaks English as well as a native speaker.

U ingliz tilida ona tilida so'zlashuvchi kabi yaxshi gapiradi.
