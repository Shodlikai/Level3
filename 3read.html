Where money has no value

Strangely, or perhaps, not very strangely, there is a place in Britain where money has no value. It's called the Royal Mint, and it's situated in South Wales. The Mint stands behind high security fences. It is not open to tourists, but as a journalist, I was able to organise a visit. Tourists can visit the recent visitor centre, next door.

In many ways, the Mint is a very odd place! As I walked round, I could see silver coins lying on the ground! Nobody stops to pick them up, they might as well be stones! Indeed, on the road outside the main building, there are streets squared into the surface! Here the streets actually are paved with money! Coins and banknotes are poured into the Royal Mint. People who go into the Royal Mint, visitors and people who work there. They are not allowed to take money in with them. They cannot take it out with them either! As a visitor, I had to leave all my money in a security locker, before I could enter. Inside the buildings, people must use special plastic coins!

The Royal Mint has been making coins for over 1,100 years, since the year 886. It's one of the oldest factories in the world! For hundreds of years, the Mint belonged to the Kings of England. Today's King has nothing to do with it, except that his head appears on all new British coins.

Money and the British Royal Mint (Continuation)

Until the 1960s, the Mint was in London, close to the Tower of London; but as it developed, the London site became too small. The Mint had to move to a bigger factory in Llantrisant, in South Wales. Today it is among the biggest coin-making factories in the world, but things are changing.

In the age of credit cards, people use fewer banknotes and fewer coins. In the 1980s, the British Royal Mint made coins for about 60 countries, including some European countries. But the last foreign coins were made in 2024. Now the Royal Mint only makes British coins - pounds, pennies, and special coins.

A new activity is being developed too. The Mint now extracts gold from old unwanted electronic goods like phones and computers. It's a very specialised job... a part of the new "circular economy".

Climate Change and Our Future

Some people say that climate change is not real. Others think it is real, but they believe we cannot stop it. A few people even say that humans are not the cause of climate change. But today, most people understand that the Earth is getting hotter, and we must do something about it. We only have one planet, and we need to take care of it.

Twenty years ago, some scientists were not sure if climate change was real. But now almost all scientists agree: the world is getting hotter because of human activity. If we do nothing, the climate will become very dangerous. However, we can still stop the worst effects if we act now.

In November 2021, leaders from many countries met in Glasgow, Scotland for the COP26 summit. They made decisions to slow down climate change. Many countries promised to stop adding more carbons to the air before 2050. Some countries want to do this even sooner. World leaders agree that we should stop using coal and oil for energy. Instead, we should use clean energy like solar power, wind power, and water power.

By the year 2100, we might get all our energy from these renewable sources. The green energy revolution has already started, but it will take a lot of time and money. Some people are afraid that it will be too expensive or too difficult.

Most people want to protect the Earth, but not everyone agrees on what to do. Some people think about themselves more than the planet. Big companies often care more about money than the environment. Some politicians do not want to make difficult decisions because they are afraid people will not like them.

Even though there are challenges, we must work together to fight climate change. Our future depends on it!

Pul va Britaniya Qirollik Zargarlik Uyi (Davomi)

1960-yillargacha Zargarlik Uyi Londonda, London minorasi yaqinida joylashgan edi; ammo u rivojlangan sari, London maydoni juda kichik bo'lib qoldi. Zargarlik Uyi Janubiy Uelsdagi Llantrisantdagi kattaroq zavodga ko'chishga majbur bo'ldi. Bugungi kunda u dunyodagi eng yirik tanga yasash zavodlari qatoriga kiradi, ammo vaziyat o'zgarmoqda.

Kredit kartalari davrida odamlar kamroq banknot va kamroq tangalardan foydalanishadi. 1980-yillarda Britaniya Qirollik Zargarlik Uyi 60 ga yaqin mamlakat uchun tanga yasagan, shu jumladan ba'zi Yevropa mamlakatlari uchun ham. Ammo oxirgi xorijiy tangalar 2024-yilda yasalgan. Hozirda Qirollik Zargarlik Uyi faqat Britaniya tangalarini - funt, pens va maxsus tangalarni yasaydi.

Yangi faoliyat ham rivojlanmoqda. Zargarlik Uyi hozirda eski keraksiz elektron tovarlardan, masalan, telefon va kompyuterlardan oltin ajratib oladi. Bu juda ixtisoslashgan ish... yangi "dumaloq iqtisodiyot"ning bir qismi.

Iqlim o'zgarishi va bizning kelajagimiz

Ba'zi odamlar iqlim o'zgarishi haqiqiy emas deydilar. Boshqalar esa u haqiqiy deb o'ylashadi, lekin uni to'xtata olmaymiz deb hisoblashadi. Hatto ba'zi odamlar iqlim o'zgarishiga insonlar sababchi emas deydilar. Ammo bugungi kunda ko'pchilik Yerning issiqroq bo'layotganini tushunadi va biz bu borada biror narsa qilishimiz kerak. Bizda faqat bitta sayyora bor va biz unga g'amxo'rlik qilishimiz kerak.

Yigirma yil oldin, ba'zi olimlar iqlim o'zgarishi haqiqiy ekanligiga ishonch hosil qilishmagan. Lekin hozir deyarli barcha olimlar bir fikrga kelishdi: inson faoliyati tufayli dunyo issiqroq bo'lmoqda. Agar hech narsa qilmasak, iqlim juda xavfli bo'ladi. Biroq, agar hozir harakat qilsak, eng yomon oqibatlarni to'xtata olamiz.

2021-yil noyabr oyida ko'plab mamlakatlar rahbarlari Shotlandiyaning Glazgoda COP26 sammitida uchrashishdi. Ular iqlim o'zgarishini sekinlashtirish bo'yicha qarorlar qabul qilishdi. Ko'plab mamlakatlar 2050-yilgacha havoga ko'proq karbon qo'shishni to'xtatishga va'da berishdi. Ba'zi mamlakatlar buni yanada tezroq qilishni xohlashadi. Dunyo rahbarlari energiya uchun ko'mir va neftdan foydalanishni to'xtatishimiz kerakligiga rozi. Buning o'rniga, biz quyosh energiyasi, shamol energiyasi va suv energiyasi kabi toza energiyadan foydalanishimiz kerak.

2100-yilga kelib, biz barcha energiyamizni ushbu qayta tiklanadigan manbalardan olishimiz mumkin. Yashil energiya inqilobi allaqachon boshlangan, ammo bu ko'p vaqt va pul talab qiladi. Ba'zi odamlar bu juda qimmat yoki juda qiyin bo'lishidan qo'rqishadi.

Ko'pchilik Yerni himoya qilishni xohlaydi, lekin nima qilish kerakligi haqida hamma ham kelisha olmaydi. Ba'zi odamlar sayyora haqida ko'proq o'zlarini o'ylashadi. Katta kompaniyalar ko'pincha atrof-muhitdan ko'proq pul haqida qayg'urishadi. Ba'zi siyosatchilar qiyin qarorlar qabul qilishni xohlamaydilar, chunki ular odamlar ularni yoqtirmasliklaridan qo'rqishadi.

Qiyinchiliklar mavjud bo'lsa-da, biz iqlim o'zgarishiga qarshi kurashish uchun birgalikda ishlashimiz kerak. Kelajagimiz bunga bog'liq!
