The Most Famous Symbol of London

What is the most famous symbol of London? Is it Big Ben? The statue of Eros in Piccadilly Circus? Or could it be something less artistic? Maybe it is the big red double-decker bus!

London's red buses are famous all over the world. People see them and immediately think of London! Tourists love them - They enjoy riding on London buses to see famous places. London buses travel the world - Sometimes they are used to advertise British events or big shops in other countries. No words are needed - Even without the words "London Transport", people still recognize them!

The red double-decker bus is not just transport—it is a symbol of London!

The Story of London's Red Buses

Over 100 years ago, on October 25, 1911, the last horse-drawn bus in London made its final journey. From that day, the famous red motor buses became the kings of the road!

Early double-decker buses had stairs at the back to reach the top. No roof for passengers on top! They only had a cloth cover to protect them from rain. It wasn't until the 1930s that all new buses had roofs over the upper deck.

Before 1907, London buses were different colors depending on the company that operated them. But in 1907, London General Omnibus Company painted all its buses red to make them easy to spot.

ENGLISH GHOST STORIES

Do you believe in ghosts? If you do, you are not alone! In Britain, there are many old castles that people say are haunted. One of the most famous haunted places is the Tower of London.

The Tower of London has a long and dark history. Many people were put in prison there, and some were even executed outside its gates. One of the most famous ghosts in the Tower is Lady Jane Grey.

Lady Jane Grey became Queen of England on July 9, 1554, when she was only 17 years old. However, another woman, Mary, also wanted to be Queen. Mary's supporters were stronger, and after just nine days, Jane was sent to the Tower of London. On July 19, Jane was executed outside the Tower.

Since then, people say that her ghost still walks through the rooms and corridors of the Tower of London!

The Haunted Village of Prestbury

Not all ghosts are famous. The village of Prestbury, in Gloucestershire, is known as one of the most haunted villages in England.

The Headless Horseman: Many people say they have seen or heard a headless horseman riding through the village on December 31st! He was said to be a soldier who fought in the English Civil War in the 17th century.

The Spinette Player: In an old cottage in Prestbury, there is a ghost called the "spinette player". At night, people sometimes hear music coming from an empty room—but no one is there!

My Own Ghost Story

A few years ago, I went to visit some friends who lived in an old house in the countryside. They didn't know I was coming, and when I arrived, they already had other visitors.

"Never mind," my friend Ella said. "You can sleep in the small guest room. We don't use it often, but it will be fine for one night." As we said goodnight, Ella added something strange: "Oh, and please lock the door before you go to bed. Otherwise, it may open by itself."

I locked the door, got into bed, and fell asleep. But I slept badly—I wasn't sure if I was awake or dreaming. Then, suddenly, I knew I was awake. I could feel hands touching my face! I tried to push them away, but there was nothing there. I quickly found the light switch and turned it on. But the room was empty.

"It was just a dream," I told myself and went back to sleep.

The next morning, I got a shock. The door, which I had locked, was now open! At breakfast, I told Ella about my dream and the open door.

"You too!" she said. "Yes, I know. That's why we don't use that bedroom. It's the blind lady!"

Ella explained that many years ago, a blind girl lived in that house. That room was hers. She died when she was about 30 years old, but since then, she keeps coming back. "She always touches the bed sheets before she gets in," Ella said. "Many visitors have had the same experience. But don't worry—she was a lovely girl. She has never hurt anyone."

At that moment, I felt the skin on my neck go cold... Since that night, I have always believed in ghosts!

Londonning eng mashhur ramzi

Londonning eng mashhur ramzi nima? Big Benmi? Piccadilly Circusdagi Eros haykalimi? Yoki u kamroq badiiy narsadirmi? Ehtimol, u katta qizil ikki qavatli avtobusdir!

Londonning qizil avtobuslari butun dunyoga mashhur. Odamlar ularni ko'rgach, darhol Londonni eslashadi! Sayyohlar ularni yaxshi ko'radilar - ular mashhur joylarni ko'rish uchun London avtobuslarida sayr qilishdan zavqlanadilar. London avtobuslari butun dunyo bo'ylab sayohat qiladi - Ba'zan ular boshqa mamlakatlarda Britaniya tadbirlari yoki yirik do'konlarni reklama qilish uchun ishlatiladi. Hech qanday so'zlar kerak emas - "London Transport" yozuvlari bo'lmasa ham, odamlar ularni taniy oladilar!

Qizil ikki qavatli avtobus nafaqat transport vositasi - u Londonning ramzidir!

London Qizil Avtobuslarining Tarixi

100 yildan ko'proq vaqt oldin, 1911-yil 25-oktabrda Londonda so'nggi ot tortgan avtobus so'nggi safarini amalga oshirdi. Shu kundan boshlab, mashhur qizil motorli avtobuslar yo'l podsholari bo'ldi!

Dastlabki ikki qavatli avtobuslarda yuqoriga chiqish uchun orqa tomonda zinapoyalar bo'lgan. Yuqori qavatda yo'lovchilar uchun tom yo'q edi! Ular faqat yomg'irdan himoya qilish uchun mato bilan qoplangan edi. 1930-yillargacha yangi avtobuslarning yuqori qavati tom bilan qoplangan bo'lmagan.

1907-yilgacha London avtobuslari ularni boshqargan kompaniyaga qarab turli xil ranglarda bo'lgan. Ammo 1907-yilda London General Omnibus Company barcha avtobuslarini qizil rangga bo'yashni boshladi, ularni osongina tanib olish uchun.

INGLIZ ARVOH HIKOYALARI

Siz arvohlarga ishonasizmi? Agar ishonsangiz, yolg'iz emassiz! Britaniyada odamlar jinlangan deb aytadigan ko'plab qadimiy qal'alar mavjud. Eng mashhur jinlangan joylardan biri London qal'asidir.

London qal'asining uzun va qorong'i tarixi bor. Ko'p odamlar u yerda qamoqqa olingan va ba'zilari darvozasi oldida qatl etilgan. Qal'adagi eng mashhur arvohlardan biri Lady Jane Grey hisoblanadi.

Lady Jane Grey 1554-yil 9-iyulda, atigi 17 yoshida Angliya qirolichasi bo'lgan. Biroq, boshqa bir ayol, Mary ham qirolicha bo'lishni xohlagan. Maryning tarafdorlari kuchliroq bo'lgan va atigi to'qqiz kun o'tgach, Jane London qal'asiga yuborilgan. 19-iyul kuni Jane qal'a oldida qatl etilgan.

Shundan beri odamlar uning arvohi hali ham London qal'asining xonalari va koridorlarida yurishini aytishadi!

Jinlangan Prestbury Qishlog'i

Barcha arvohlar mashhur emas. Gloucestershiredagi Prestbury qishlog'i Angliyadagi eng ko'p jinlangan qishloqlardan biri sifatida tanilgan.

Boshsiz Otliq: Ko'p odamlar 31-dekabr kuni qishloq bo'ylab boshsiz otliqni ko'rgan yoki eshitganliklarini aytishadi! U 17-asrda Angliya Fuqarolar urushida jang qilgan askar bo'lgan deyiladi.

Spinette Chaluvchi: Prestburydagi qadimgi uyda "spinette chaluvchi" deb nomlangan arvoh bor. Tunda odamlar ba'zan bo'sh xonadan musiqani eshitishadi - lekin u yerda hech kim yo'q!

Mening Shaxsiy Arvoh Hikoyam

Bir necha yil oldin, men qishloq joydagi qadimgi uyda yashovchi do'stlarimga tashrif buyurgan edim. Ular mening kelishimni bilishmagan va men kelganimda, ularning boshqa mehmonlari bor edi.

"Hech qisi yo'q," - dedi do'stim Ella. "Siz kichik mehmon xonasida uxlashingiz mumkin. Biz uni tez-tez ishlatmaymiz, lekin bir kechaga yaraydi." Xayrlashganda, Ella g'alati narsa qo'shib qo'ydi: "Oh, va iltimos, yotishdan oldin eshikni qulflang. Aks holda, u o'z-o'zidan ochilishi mumkin."

Men eshikni qulfladim, yotgan joyga joylashdim va uxladim. Lekin yomon uxladim - men uyog'animmi yoki tush ko'rayotganimni bilmay qoldim. Keyin, to'satdan, men uyog'animni angladim. Yuzimga tegayotgan qo'llarni his qila oldim! Men ularni itarishga harakat qildim, lekin u yerda hech narsa yo'q edi. Men tezda chiroq tugmasini topdim va yoqdim. Lekin xona bo'sh edi.

"Bu shunchaki tush edi," - deb o'zimga aytdim va yana uxladim.

Ertalab men hayratga tushdim. Men qulflagan eshik endi ochiq edi! Nonushta paytida men Ellaga tushim va ochiq eshik haqida aytdim.

"Siz ham!" - dedi u. "Ha, men bilaman. Shuning uchun biz bu yotoq xonasidan foydalanmaymiz. Bu ko'r qiz!"

Ella tushuntirdiki, ko'p yillar oldin, bu uyda ko'r qiz yashagan. Bu xona uniki bo'lgan. U taxminan 30 yoshida vafot etgan, ammo shundan beri u doimo qaytib keladi. "U doimo yotgan joyga joylashishdan oldin choyshablarga tegadi," - dedi Ella. "Ko'p mehmonlar bir xil tajribaga ega bo'lishgan. Lekin tashvishlanmang - u yoqimli qiz edi. U hech kimga zarar yetkazmagan."

O'sha paytda men bo'ynimdagi terning sovib ketganini his qildim... O'sha kundan beri men har doim arvohlarga ishonaman!
